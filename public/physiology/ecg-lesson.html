Measure the heart rate

Tap two neighbouring R waves on the strip. One small box is 1 mm, which is 0.04 s at 25 mm/s.

Rate formulas1500 ÷ RR in small boxes. Or 300 ÷ large boxes. For an irregular rhythm, count R waves in 6 s and multiply by 10.

What each lead sees

Quick check: look at leads I and aVF. Both upright means normal axis. I upright and aVF downward means left axis deviation. I downward and aVF upright means right axis deviation.
